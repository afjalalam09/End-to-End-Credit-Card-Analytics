# End-to-End Credit Card Analytics Dashboard 📊

## 🎯 Project Objective
The primary goal of this project is to develop a comprehensive and interactive Credit Card Financial Dashboard using **SQL** and **Power BI**. This dashboard provides real-time business insights into key performance metrics, customer demographics, and transactional trends, enabling stakeholders to optimize credit card operations and marketing strategies.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack Used
*   **Database Management:** SQL (Data Extraction, Table Creation, EDA)
*   **Business Intelligence:** Power BI (Data Modeling, DAX, Dashboard Design)
*   **Data Processing:** Advanced Excel / CSV

## 📂 Repository Structure
*   **`Data/`**: Contains raw datasets (`credit_card.csv`, `customer.csv`).
*   **`SQL_Queries/`**: Includes `01_database_setup_and_queries.sql` for table schema, CSV imports, and exploratory data analysis.
*   **`Dashboard/`**: Contains the interactive `Credit_Card_Analytics_Dashboard.pbix` file and a quick-view `Credit_Card_Analytics_Report.pdf`.
*   **`Images/`**: Screenshots of the final dashboards for quick reference.

## 📈 Key Business Insights

### 1. Transaction & Financial Performance
*   **Overall Revenue & Volume:** The credit card portfolio generated a total revenue of **$55M**, with **$7.84M** in total interest earned from **656K** total transactions.
*   **Hero Product:** The **Blue Card** is the highest-performing product, contributing to the vast majority of the revenue (~$46M), followed by the Silver Card.
*   **Transaction Behavior:** Customers prefer physical usage, with **Swipe** transactions leading the revenue generation ($35M+), followed closely by Chip usage. 
*   **Expenditure Trends:** The highest volume of credit card spending is concentrated in the **Bills** and **Entertainment** categories.

### 2. Customer Demographics & Targeting
*   **Income & Job Profiles:** Customers in the **Businessman** and **White-collar** job categories generate the highest revenue. The overall customer base boasts a total income of **$576M**.
*   **Geographical Hotspots:** The top 3 revenue-generating states are **Texas (TX), New York (NY), and California (CA)**, making them prime locations for targeted marketing campaigns.
*   **Age & Demographics:** The highest revenue contribution comes from the **40-50** and **50-60** age groups, indicating a mature and financially stable primary user base. 
*   **Customer Satisfaction:** The current Customer Satisfaction Score (CSS) sits at **3.19**, indicating a moderate satisfaction level with room for service optimization.

1.  **Quick View:** Open the `Dashboard/Credit_Card_Analytics_Report.pdf` to see the static snapshots of the dashboards.
